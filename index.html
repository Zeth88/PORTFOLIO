About Me

I'm Rahul Kujur — a graphic designer who believes the best brands feel inevitable, not invented.

Over the past eight years I've worked with founders, marketing teams, and agencies to build visual identities that hold up across a billboard, a feed, and a business card. My process starts with research, not templates — understanding what a brand actually needs to say before deciding how it should look.

Today I split my time between brand identity systems, campaign design, and motion — and I'm just as happy sketching a logo mark as I am animating it for a launch reel.

FAQ

Good to know.

What's your typical turnaround time?

Most brand identity projects take 3–5 weeks depending on scope; single assets like posters usually take 3–7 days.

Do you work with startups on a budget?

Yes — I offer scaled-down identity packages for early-stage startups, with room to expand as you grow.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Definitely. I regularly collaborate with in-house marketing and product teams, slotting into existing workflows.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief, any existing brand materials, and a sense of your timeline and budget — I'll take it from there.
